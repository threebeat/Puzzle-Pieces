# Visual references for the south-hall revision

All new paintings, geometry and material maps are original procedural work. These sources informed shape, construction and finish; their images are not bundled with the game.

| Reference | Applied observation |
| --- | --- |
| [The Met — Diadoumenos](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/251838) | A weight-bearing leg, relaxed opposite leg, and opposed shoulder/hip tilt inform the athlete and bearer studies. |
| [The Met — draped woman](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/247004) | Narrow vertical pleats beneath broader diagonal cloak folds inform the two draped figures. |
| [The Met — philosopher portrait](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/252887) | Deep-set eyes, textured hair and a bearded face inform the portrait bust. |
| [Oklahoma State Extension — Boston fern](https://extension.okstate.edu/programs/plant-id/plant-profiles/boston-fern) | Arched fronds with dense, paired leaflets replace broad generic leaves. |
| [KONE — materials and accessories](https://www.kone.us/Images/8717%20KONE%20materials%20and%20accessories-lr_tcm25-19069.pdf) | Directional brushed metal, center-opening panels, dark reveals, sill tracks and clean control plates inform the lifts. |
| [Pilkington — shopfront glazing](https://www.pilkington.com/en/global/commercial-applications/glass-applications/shopfront) | Large transparent panes with narrow frames and visible edges inform the gift-shop curtain wall. |
| User-supplied reception photograph | Curved counter edges, pale translucent surface, vertical wood front and work left on the counter inform the central information desk. |

The figures are contemporary fictional artworks made for this game, not scans or reproductions of the museum objects. The visual proofs use native Canvas and geometry projection; they do not establish browser lighting, WebGL performance or audio balance.
